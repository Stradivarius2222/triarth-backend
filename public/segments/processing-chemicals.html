Processing Chemicals

We offer a wide range of specialty chemicals designed to optimize industrial processes, enhance product quality, and improve operational efficiency. Our portfolio includes functional chemical solutions tailored to meet the diverse needs of various industries, supporting applications such as process optimization, performance enhancement, and product quality improvement. With our commitment to quality and reliable supply, we work closely with our partners and customers to identify the right solutions for their specific processing needs, helping them achieve consistent results and greater operational efficiency.

Metalworking Fluid

Offering specialty chemicals and customized blends to help Metalworking Fluid formulators create final products that are tailored to their specific needs with durable and high-performance solutions. We provide specialty additives for niche application areas in anti-foam, corrosion inhibitor, emulsifier, wetting agent, lubricant, biocide, and preservative.

Battery

Providing specialty additives for niche application areas in the battery manufacturing and processing.

Textile Formulator

Offering specialty chemicals for different steps of textile processing preparation, mercerization, bleaching, pre-treatment, finishing, washing off, and after-treatment, which includes specialty surfactants, levelling agents, and various softeners.

Water Treatment

Providing additives for high performance solutions in a broad case of applications for industrial water treatment.

Crop Care Formulator

Specialty surfactants, customized blends, and ingredients designed for Crop Care to help farmers prevent weeds, fungi, and pests, which destroy their crops.
